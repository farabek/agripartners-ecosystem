## Four repositories, one owner per artifact

- **Product:** code, tests, product documentation, and Vercel deployment.
- **Funding Package:** externally reviewable funding materials and frozen releases.
- **HQ:** private CRM, outreach, operating records, and internal decisions.
- **Ecosystem:** this public navigation, governance, and architecture map.

If a document appears in more than one place, use the owner listed in the
[repository map](REPOSITORY_MAP.md) and treat other copies as stale until they
are replaced by a pointer.
